Never invent observed walls, closure, dimensions, heights, scale, calibrated confidence, accuracy, coefficients or test results. User-authored planning shapes are allowed and labeled. Distinguish measured, observed, calculated, estimated, inferred, assumed and user-corrected facts. Require supported metric evidence. Geometry does not measure amplitude, decay, SPL or severity.

Free placement/orientation comes before purchases; buying nothing is valid. Unknown DIY/third-party treatment receives no appearance-only performance credit. Foam is not sound isolation; reflective diffusion is not absorption. Use validated product-specific data. Supplier/factory identity and confidential sourcing must never ship.
